Who we are

We here at Steinhaus Farm — Stone House Farm — love to grow, produce, and share simple, clean food. We also like to build with stone.

We (Henry and Grit) found each other in the Finger Lakes and started a small family and farm. Our house and sugar shack are built by hand out of field stone.

Henry

Builder of all Things, Sugar Master & Vegetable Whisperer

He is an American vagabond, who hopped trains all around the country, and learned to farm in Nebraska and the Finger Lakes. He loves to build things out of stone and wood, he is very passionate about growing clean food, and producing maple syrup.

Grit

Creature Management & Response Team

She is a German language teacher with an interest in life science. She has fond childhood memories of her grandparents, who lived in the country. She loves to manage the sheep, and handle the paperwork.

The Children

Good Mood Committee & Future Farm Hands

Our children and their future are one of our biggest motivations to do what we do.

Schnecke

Security Detail & Eater of All Things

She is a rescued Cattle Dog – Catahoula mix, who takes her job very seriously, sometimes too seriously…

Stay with us

A German teacher and an American vagabond built this stone house by hand in the Finger Lakes. Scandinavian modern loft meets working homestead — radiant floors, high ceilings, amazing sunsets over Smith Valley. Taste our homemade maple syrup, meet the sheep, and unplug in a place unlike anywhere else. Perfectly located between Ithaca and Watkins Glen.
